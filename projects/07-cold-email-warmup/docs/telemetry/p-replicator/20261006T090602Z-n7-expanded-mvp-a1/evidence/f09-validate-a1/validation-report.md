## F09-V1 — HIGH: expiry does not prove previous socket termination
Affected: AC-f09-live-transport-007; 02_pseudocode.md Data Structures TransportOperation and Algorithm: Own bounded resources steps1/5; 03_architecture.md Data Architecture/Security Architecture; 04_refinement.md Performance.

The plan automatically reclaims an expired120s slot and promises globally at most2SMTP/4IMAP and one operation per protocol/mailbox. Its proposed safety argument is a<=90s owner deadline and abort before a suspended owner resumes writing. Counterexample: owner A establishes its socket, is OS-suspended or its event loop stalls past120s, and owner B reclaims A's expired slot and opens another socket for that mailbox. A's socket remains open while A cannot run its timer/cleanup. Slot ownership fences prevent stale DB publication, but cannot close a socket in another paused process. Abort on resume occurs after the overlap. The six-row counter therefore bounds current leases, not all still-open operations. This is a design contradiction in the explicitly required suspension/recovery behavior, not an invented demand for a new SLO.

The pinned [Node22.20.0 timers contract](https://nodejs.org/download/release/v22.20.0/docs/api/timers.html#scheduling-timers), checked2026-10-06, states callback timing depends on other event-loop work. Thus the lease margin alone is not a termination proof. This conclusion is an inference from that runtime contract and the stated reclaim algorithm; no runtime test failure is claimed.

Required narrow correction: expiry marks an occupied slot unavailable for new I/O until identity-fenced socket-close acknowledgement or confirmed termination of its owning process establishes safe release. Specify the minimal existing-architecture cleanup/recovery mechanism, including restart/DB failure behavior; do not silently weaken the global/per-mailbox bound to a count of unexpired rows. If a supervisor is selected, it must confirm owner termination before reclaim; merely sending an abort/kill request is insufficient. Add a real multi-process suspended-owner witness: hold a socket, suspend owner past lease, attempt same-slot/mailbox admission, verify no overlap; terminate/close owner and prove admission resumes. Retain unknown_delivery/no-retry for any committed SMTP attempt. No generic orchestrator or validator is requested.

Disposition: NEEDS_WORK until the targeted PLAN correction has its own source-bound revalidation. Frozen source cannot be declared READY merely because the structural checker passes.
